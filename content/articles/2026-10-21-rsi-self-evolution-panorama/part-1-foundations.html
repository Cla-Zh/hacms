学术基础与理论框架

自主度分级、验证强度层级、三条算法范式与 Gödel 谱系

Autonomy Levels, Verification Strength Hierarchy, Three Algorithmic Paradigms and the Gödel Lineage

这一部分回答一个问题：当我们说"AI 在自我改进"时，我们在说哪一件事？2026 年出现了三套互不相同但都在使用的坐标轴，它们给出的答案并不一致——理解这种不一致本身就是理解当前领域的关键。

This section answers one question: when we say "AI is self-improving," which of several distinct things do we mean? Three different coordinate systems emerged in 2026 and they do not agree. Understanding that disagreement is itself key to understanding the field.

三套坐标轴 / Three Coordinate Systems

1.1 自主度分级：L1–L5Autonomy Levels: L1–L5

《人类建造的最后一个 AI》这篇 34 位作者的综述提出了一个 Headroom-Closed Index（HCI）用于诊断现有大模型能力进步的不均衡，并给出一个五级自主度路线图（原文以 B0 为该框架中未展开的基线层级，本文的讨论自 L1 起）。它的 RSI 定义值得逐字引用："一个自主闭环过程：AI 系统识别自身局限、开发并验证改进、再用所得能力去改进改进过程本身。"

The 34-author survey "The Last AI Built by Humans" proposes a Headroom-Closed Index (HCI) to diagnose the unevenness of current LLM capability progress, and offers a five-level autonomy roadmap. Its definition of RSI is worth quoting verbatim: "an autonomous closed-loop process where an AI system identifies its own limitations, develops and validates improvements, then uses the resulting capability to improve the improvement process itself."

1.2 双轴分类法：改进什么 × 闭环程度Dual-Axis Taxonomy: What × Closure

另一篇 2026 年 7 月的综述系统映射了 1,250 篇 arXiv 论文（2024–2026），提出双轴分类法：轴一是"系统改进什么"（部署期行为 / 训练期策略 / 评估器本身 / 研究过程），轴二是"闭环程度"（human-in-the-loop / human-on-the-loop / fully closed）。它做的核心切分是有界自我精炼与开放式 RSI 的区分：前者有外部固定评估器锚定、因此收敛；后者没有固定外部锚点、原则上发散。

A separate July 2026 survey systematically mapped 1,250 arXiv papers (2024–2026) and proposed a dual-axis taxonomy: axis one, "what the system improves" (deployment-time behavior / training-time policy / the evaluator itself / the research process); axis two, "degree of closure" (human-in-the-loop / human-on-the-loop / fully closed). Its core cut is bounded self-refinement versus open-ended RSI: the former is anchored by a fixed external evaluator and therefore converges; the latter has no fixed external anchor and is divergent in principle.

Source: arXiv:2607.07663

一个诚实但重要的限定 / An honest but important qualification

该综述对"无外部反馈的递归自评"给出了量化观察：三个供应商的模型在四类任务上跑 10 轮无接地的自我批评迭代，信息变化量下降 55%；而只需一次最小接地干预（一次外部验证）即可改变结果。结论措辞是："无外部反馈的递归自评产生的是改写，而非进展。"

This survey offers a quantitative observation on ungrounded recursive self-evaluation: across four task types, models from three providers ran 10 rounds of self-critique without grounding, with information variation falling 55%; a single minimal grounding intervention (one external validation) changed the outcome. Its phrasing: ungrounded recursive self-evaluation produces rewriting, not progress.

Source: arXiv:2607.07663 · 二手转引，confidence=medium

1.3 权重与脚手架：改进对象的二分法Weights vs Scaffold: The Object of Improvement

一篇 2026 年 7 月的 97 页综述（末位作者是 Gödel Machine 的原始提出者 Jürgen Schmidhuber）把现代智能体建模为"基础模型 + 运行脚手架"的耦合配置，并把自我改进形式化为自诱发更新算子：系统获取并提交对模型参数 θ 或脚手架 Σ 的更新。这个二分法可以直接用作整篇综述的组织轴，而且它追溯到了 Schmidhuber 1987/1993 的显式自指元学习。

A 97-page July 2026 survey (whose last author is Jürgen Schmidhuber, the original proposer of the Gödel Machine) models modern agents as a coupled configuration of "foundation model plus operational scaffold" and formalizes self-improvement as a self-induced update operator: the system acquires and submits updates to model parameters θ or to the scaffold Σ. This dichotomy can serve as the organizing axis for a whole survey, and it traces back to Schmidhuber's 1987/1993 explicit self-referential meta-learning.

Source: arXiv:2607.13104 · 项目页 selfimproving-agent.github.io · 清单 awesome-Self-Improving-Agents

验证强度层级：本文的主坐标轴The Verification Strength Hierarchy: This Article's Main Axis

那篇 1,250 篇文献的系统综述还提出了一条本文反复使用的经验规律：已演示的自改进强度沿着验证信号的可靠性层级排列。层级从强到弱是：形式化验证器、外部接地评估、学习式评判器或过程奖励模型、内生自评。分布统计也印证了关注度的不均衡：自评估类 318 篇（自改进强度 82%）、自动研究 139 篇（76%），而"基础、极限与安全"只有 60 篇（57%）。

That same 1,250-paper systematic survey also supplies the empirical regularity this article returns to: demonstrated self-improvement strength is ordered along the reliability hierarchy of the improvement signal. From strongest to weakest: formal verifiers, externally grounded evaluation, learned judges or process reward models, endogenous self-evaluation. The distribution also reveals an imbalance in attention: self-evaluation 318 papers (82% self-improvement strength), auto-research 139 (76%), versus "foundations, limits & safety" at just 60 (57%).

Gödel 谱系：从理论构想到 L5The Gödel Lineage: From Theoretical Concept to L5

现代自改进智能体的思想谱系可以清晰追溯。Schmidhuber 在 1987 与 1993 年提出 Gödel Machine——一个理论上能修改自己学习算法的自指框架。真正把这一思想变成可运行系统的是 2025 年 ACL 主会论文《Gödel Agent》，它用 monkey patching 实现完全自指：同时修改策略 π 与元学习算法 I，公式为 πt+1, It+1 = It(πt, It, rt, g)。

The lineage of modern self-improving agents is traceable. Schmidhuber proposed the Gödel Machine in 1987 and 1993—a self-referential framework theoretically able to modify its own learning algorithm. The work that turned this into a running system is the ACL 2025 main-conference paper "Gödel Agent", implemented via monkey patching as a fully self-referential agent that modifies both the policy π and the meta-learning algorithm I: πt+1, It+1 = It(πt, It, rt, g).

三条算法范式Three Algorithmic Paradigms

范式一：权重级强化学习Paradigm 1: Weight-Level RL

AlphaTensor 把矩阵乘法算法发现形式化为单人博弈 TensorGame，基于 AlphaZero 构建神经网络加蒙特卡洛树搜索的深度强化学习智能体。它的独特之处在于解的正确性由数学本身可证明地保证——找到低秩分解即可证明乘法次数。这让它天然位于验证强度层级的最顶层。动作空间超过 1012，比围棋或国际象棋的数百个动作大得多。

AlphaTensor formulates matrix multiplication algorithm discovery as the single-player game TensorGame, building a deep RL agent on neural networks plus MCTS atop AlphaZero. Its distinctiveness is that the correctness of a solution is provable by the mathematics itself—finding a low-rank decomposition proves the multiplication count. This places it naturally at the top of the verification hierarchy. Its action space exceeds 1012, vastly larger than the hundreds of actions in Go or chess.

Source: Nature 610:47-53 · github.com/google-deepmind/alphatensor

同一范式在 2025 年扩展到量子领域。AlphaTensor-Quantum 把 T-count 最小化转化为对称张量的低秩 Waring 分解，在一组算术基准上超过既有方法，发现了类似 Karatsuba 的有限域乘法算法，并在 Shor 算法与量子化学模拟所用的算术计算上达到人类手工最优解。一份独立的可复用性报告给出了更冷静的判断：小规模复现可行但需调超参，扩展到不同量子比特数无需重训，但计算开销高，"可能只有对被大量应用复用的重要量子线路原语才划算"。

The same paradigm extended to quantum in 2025. AlphaTensor-Quantum reduces T-count minimization to low-rank Waring decomposition of symmetric tensors, surpassing existing methods on a set of arithmetic benchmarks, discovering finite-field multiplication algorithms resembling Karatsuba, and matching human hand-optimal solutions for arithmetic used in Shor's algorithm and quantum chemistry simulation. An independent reusability report offers a cooler assessment: small-scale replication is feasible but needs hyperparameter tuning, generalizing to different qubit counts requires no retraining, but computational cost is high and "may only pay off for important quantum circuit primitives reused at scale."

Sources: Nature Mach. Intell. 7:374-385 · 可复用性报告 Nature Mach. Intell. 8:113-117

范式二：代码级进化搜索Paradigm 2: Code-Level Evolutionary Search

这条路线把候选解表示为整个代码文件，由大模型生成、批评、进化，配合自动评估器。AlphaEvolve 相对其前身 FunSearch 的升级很能说明问题：从单函数到整文件、10–20 行到数百行、仅 Python 到任意语言、每题数百万样本到数千样本、单指标到多指标同时优化。它最惊人的结果是 4×4 复数矩阵乘法用 48 次标量乘法——白皮书称这是 56 年来该问题的首次改进。

This line represents candidate solutions as entire code files, generated, critiqued and evolved by a large model with an automated evaluator. AlphaEvolve's improvements over its predecessor FunSearch are instructive: from single function to whole file, 10–20 lines to hundreds, Python-only to any language, millions of samples per problem to thousands, single-metric to simultaneous multi-objective optimization. Its most striking result is 4×4 complex matrix multiplication in 48 scalar multiplications—which the white paper calls the first improvement to that problem in 56 years.

Source: arXiv:2506.13131

这条路线的样本效率在 2025 年被显著改善。ShinkaEvolve 在圆堆积这一经典任务上仅用 150 个样本就发现新的 SOTA 解，比既有框架"少几个数量级"，并且超过了 AlphaEvolve 在该任务上的解。这说明开放式发现存在一个可累积的公共基准——后人的起点高于前人。开源生态也在跟进：CodeEvolve 用开源 Qwen3-Coder-30B 在两个圆堆积实例上超过 AlphaEvolve，成本约低 10 倍。

This line's sample efficiency was markedly improved in 2025. ShinkaEvolve found a new SOTA solution on circle packing using only 150 samples—"orders of magnitude fewer than prior frameworks"—and surpassed AlphaEvolve's solution on that task. This shows open-ended discovery has an accumulable shared baseline: successors start higher than predecessors. The open-source ecosystem follows: CodeEvolve surpasses AlphaEvolve on two circle-packing instances with open-weight Qwen3-Coder-30B at roughly one-tenth the cost.

Sources: arXiv:2509.19349 (ShinkaEvolve, ICLR 2026) · arXiv:2510.14150 (CodeEvolve)

范式三：世界模型引导Paradigm 3: World-Model-Guided

这条路线直面 RSI 的两个具体瓶颈：外部反馈昂贵，且策略演化会让世界模型预测失效——策略更新改变候选动作与经验分布，世界模型更新又改变候选排序与学习信号，二者互相污染。RSI-Anything 用预测式世界模型指导动作选择与学习数据选择，把持久改进提升到"策略—世界模型对"这一系统层级。在代码修复任务上（编码器固定、同等在线测试用量），静态验证器 26.3% → 预测式选择 29.2% → 世界模型自适应后 31.2%，而在线测试调用数基本相同（3.72 vs 3.71）。

This line confronts two concrete RSI bottlenecks: external feedback is expensive, and policy evolution invalidates world-model predictions—policy updates shift the candidate action and experience distribution while world-model updates shift candidate ranking and learning signal, mutually polluting each other. RSI-Anything uses a predictive world model to guide action selection and learning-data selection, elevating persistent improvement to the system level of "policy–world-model pairs." On code repair (coder fixed, equal online test budget): static verifier 26.3% → predictive selection 29.2% → after world-model adaptation 31.2%, with online test calls essentially unchanged (3.72 vs 3.71).

Source: arXiv:2610.13912

Dream-RSI 给出了一个更反直觉的诊断：RSI 的具体瓶颈是探索策略本身的管理与改进，而不是探索能力。它的洞见是，累积的发现历史本身可以充当已实现搜索空间上的 replay simulator——在由历史发现树构成的"梦境"里给探索策略提供即时、廉价的 off-policy 反馈。改进后的策略再上线部署并反过来扩充 simulator 池。需要指出的是，该论文并未证明 replay 节省了墙钟时间或总计算量。

Dream-RSI offers a more counterintuitive diagnosis: the concrete RSI bottleneck is the management and improvement of the exploration policy itself, not exploration capability. Its insight is that accumulated discovery history can itself serve as a replay simulator over the realized search space—providing the exploration policy with immediate, cheap off-policy feedback in a "dream" built from the historical discovery tree. Improved policies then deploy online and in turn expand the simulator pool. Notably, the paper does not prove replay saves wall-clock time or total compute.

Source: arXiv:2609.14858 · 限定条件 confidence=medium

零数据自博弈的两个变体Two Variants of Zero-Data Self-Play

Absolute Zero 与 R-Zero 代表了同一个方向的两种取舍。Absolute Zero 更彻底：不仅不用监督微调、不用任何外部提示或答案数据，连训练任务本身也是模型自己提出的。单一模型同时扮演提议者与求解者，用代码执行器既验证自提出的代码推理任务又验证答案，形成统一的可验证奖励源。

Absolute Zero and R-Zero represent two trade-offs along the same direction. Absolute Zero is more thorough: not only no supervised fine-tuning and no external prompts or answer data, the training tasks themselves are model-proposed. A single model plays proposer and solver, with a code executor validating both self-proposed code-reasoning tasks and answers, forming a single verifiable reward source.

Source: arXiv:2505.03335 (NeurIPS 2025)

R-Zero 从单个基座模型初始化两个独立角色：挑战者负责提出任务，求解者负责解决。挑战者的奖励激励它提出位于求解者当前能力边缘（最大不确定性）的任务，这个"边缘难度"设计有理论动机——每轮生成最大效率的课程。但 R-Zero 没有外部执行器，用多数投票产生伪标签，因此在验证强度层级上比 Absolute Zero 低一档。

R-Zero initializes two independent roles from a single base model: a Challenger proposing tasks and a Solver solving them. The Challenger is rewarded for proposing tasks at the Solver's maximum-uncertainty frontier, an "edge difficulty" design with theoretical motivation—each round generates a maximally efficient curriculum. But R-Zero has no external executor, using majority voting for pseudo-labels, placing it one tier below Absolute Zero on the verification hierarchy.

Source: arXiv:2508.05004 (ICLR 2026)

一个值得注意的规模效应 / A notable scaling effect

Absolute Zero 在分布外总提升上呈现清晰的规模效应：3B 模型 +5.7，7B 模型 +10.2，14B 模型 +13.2。更反直觉的是"代码先验放大推理"现象：基础 Qwen-Coder-7B 的数学分比 Qwen-7B 低 3.6 分，但经 AZR 训练后编码器变体反超基础模型 0.7 分。也就是说，代码预训练原本带来的数学劣势，在自博弈过程中被逆转了。

Absolute Zero shows a clean scaling effect on out-of-distribution total gains: 3B +5.7, 7B +10.2, 14B +13.2. More counterintuitive is "code priors amplify reasoning": base Qwen-Coder-7B scores 3.6 points below Qwen-7B on math, yet after AZR training the coder variant overtakes the base model by 0.7 points. The math disadvantage that code pretraining introduced was reversed during self-play.

小结 / Summary of Part 1

三套坐标轴的差异不是学术分歧，而是它们在回答不同的三个问题：自主度分级回答"改到了什么程度"，双轴分类法回答"改的对象与闭环程度"，验证强度层级回答"改动的正确性如何被判定"。当三者交叉时会得到一个统一图景：2025 年的突破集中在"验证极强但自主度中等"的象限（L2 + 形式化验证器）；2026 年的前沿正在向"验证中等但自主度极高"的象限移动（L5 + 评估器协同进化）。而第 4 部分将展示，这个移动会遇到三个实证刹车。

The differences among the three coordinate systems are not academic disagreement—they answer three different questions. The autonomy levels answer "how far did it get changed"; the dual-axis taxonomy answers "what is the object of change and how closed is the loop"; the verification hierarchy answers "how is the correctness of a change determined." Crossed, they yield a unified picture: 2025's breakthroughs cluster in the "very strong verification but moderate autonomy" quadrant (L2 + formal verifier); the 2026 frontier is moving toward "moderate verification but very high autonomy" (L5 + co-evolving evaluators). Part 4 will show that this move encounters three empirically-grounded brakes.
